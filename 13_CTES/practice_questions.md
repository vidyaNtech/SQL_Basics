# SQL CTE Practice Questions

## Easy

### 1. High Scorers
Create a CTE to display students who scored more than 80 marks.

---

### 2. Average Marks
Create a CTE to calculate the average marks of all students.

---

### 3. Student Count
Create a CTE to count the number of students in each department.

---

### 4. Highest Marks
Create a CTE to find the highest marks obtained by students.

---

### 5. High Salary
Create a CTE to display employees whose salary is greater than 50000.

---

## Medium

### 6. Above Average Students
Using a CTE, display students whose marks are greater than the average marks of all students.

---

### 7. Department Average
Create a CTE to calculate the average salary of each department.

---

### 8. High-Performing Departments
Using a CTE, display departments whose average student marks are greater than 80.

---

### 9. Multiple CTEs
Create two CTEs:

1. Find the number of employees in each department.
2. Find departments having more than 5 employees.

---

### 10. CTE with JOIN
Create a CTE that displays:

- Student name
- Department name
- Student marks

Use the Students and Departments tables.

---

## Advanced

### 11. Top 5 Salaries
Use a CTE to find the five employees with the highest salaries.

---

### 12. Department Salary
Create a CTE that calculates the total salary paid by each department.

Then display the department with the highest total salary.

---

### 13. Multiple CTEs with JOIN
Create one CTE to calculate the average salary of each department.

Create another CTE to retrieve department names.

Join the two CTEs and display:

- Department name
- Average salary

---

### 14. Recursive CTE
Create a recursive CTE that generates numbers from 1 to 10.

---

### 15. CTE vs Subquery
Solve the following problem using both a CTE and a subquery:

Display employees whose salary is greater than the average salary.

---
